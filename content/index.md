Play | Biology | #Psychology | Language | #Storytelling | #Art | #Technology | #Culture | #Economy | #Society intertwine in unpredictable ways. At their intersection, meaning emerges as much from the participants’ interpersonal | parasocial relationships, and from the work | memories | #Feelings | #Ideas, as it does from chance | entropy | synchronicity | pareidolia | the #Creator’s | the #Critic’s | the participant’s intent.

The writings collected here are a metonymy of branching paths—each leading somewhere, or nowhere, or back again.
