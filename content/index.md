Play | Biology | #Psychology | Language | Storytelling | #Art | #Technology | #Culture | #Economy | #Society intertwine in unpredictable ways. At their intersection, meaning emerges as much from the participants’ interpersonal | parasocial relationships, and from the work | memories | feelings | ideas, as it does from chance | entropy | synchronicity | pareidolia | the author’s | the critic’s | the participant’s intent.

The writings collected here are a metonymy of branching paths—each leading somewhere, or nowhere, or back again.
